# Contributing data

1. Identify a public source, exact endpoint/query scope and provider release/revision. Confirm access and retention separately from redistribution. Supply a rights evidence URL and required source attribution. Unknown license records remain held.
2. Capture a bounded response. Do not send private data, credentials, signed URLs or authorization headers. Mark injected or derived fixtures honestly. Keep original response bytes and report partial pagination; provider search totals are not material coverage.
3. Stage the capture and inspect every flagged record, raw hash, source field and numeric string. Propose identities separately from source entries. Do not reinterpret computed values as experiments, substitute formula matching for identity review, or infer missing state/uncertainty.
4. Supply contributor identity, source authorship, conflicts, parser/profile/code version, query, baseline, candidate IDs/hashes, rights evidence, evidence lane and limitations. New provider families/mappings need independent scientific-method and schema review before implementation.
5. A separate unconflicted reviewer inspects the exact subset, source fidelity and export rights and records their reference, rationale and IDs with `review`. The local tool checks content binding and different reviewer/contributor labels; it cannot authenticate those people. The release operator must verify identity/authority outside the tool and supply the exact trusted decision digest. Demo identities are not real approvers.
6. Build the candidate twice; compare `dataset.tar` bytes and `SHA256SUMS`. Check exact included IDs, source/property/identity counts, citation metadata and license ledger. A build does not publish or admit anything to production.
7. Corrections create a new version. Record source notice, reason, before/after identifiers, expected impact and supersession reference. Use `status-event` for withdrawals, supersession or errata. Withdrawn/superseded source IDs are blocked from new reviews/builds in this store; historical archives stay unchanged. The MVP deliberately has no automatic reinstatement operation.

## Required contribution record

- Contributor and reviewer references; independent review/COI disclosure
- Public source URL, record IDs, revision, exact query, captured byte hashes
- Evidence lane and source subtype; original numeric strings and field locators
- Known conditions/units/frames and explicit unknowns
- Access, retention, derived-export and redistribution decisions; evidence URLs
- Requested exact candidate subset and hashes; rejected/held IDs and reasons
- Dataset version/title/verified creators; code/parser/schema/profile versions
- Reproduction command and observed test results; clear not-run checks

No contribution template or provider declaration is itself approval. No fake DOI, author, ORCID or release date is acceptable. Publication requires a separately authorized repository/account workflow.
