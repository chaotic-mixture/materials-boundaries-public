# Snapshot and review policy

Capture is explicit and bounded; no scheduler or network activity starts at import. Raw capture records bytes, SHA-256, retrieval event, query, selected headers and profile/parser/code identity. Raw bytes are append-only. Cache identity includes raw content, profile, implementation, query and baseline. New retrievals retain independent event lineage even when scientific content is stable.

Staging never admits canonical materials. Review must enumerate an exact subset and bind source, candidate, context and decision digests. Unknown rights/evidence lanes and withdrawals hold records. Fixtures can only produce local_demo output. License declarations do not establish redistribution rights. Review identities and independence must be verified outside this local tool.

Dataset versions are explicit SemVer, distinct from software/schema versions; eligibility is human policy. Reusing a version for changed bytes fails. Errata/withdrawal/supersession records are detached; historical release bytes remain. Missing bounded-query results are not retractions. Correction reopening is not implemented.

Retain the acquisition store with releases for complete raw replay. Release archives exclude raw capture blobs. Relative logical references and hashes identify required replay objects; no private filesystem paths belong in metadata. There is no raw bundle exporter, remote backup, retention scheduler or DOI registration. Citation fields remain unregistered and parent_snapshot remains null.
