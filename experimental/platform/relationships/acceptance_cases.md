# Acceptance cases

## Prior design-only validation (not rerun in this integration)

The separate original design validation report recorded schema and graph integrity, exact references/revisions, endpoint types, functional-evidence gate and DAG checks. Six injected failures were rejected: duplicate identity, dangling endpoint, wrong endpoint type, unsupported functional upgrade, wrong revision and missing evidence. Count fixtures preserve 1,057 production identities, three proposed companion identities and six existing 2D observation IDs.

## Required semantic/integration tests before implementation

1. The AlN-based ceramic appears in inorganic and ceramic views but contributes one global identity. No verified semiconductor membership follows.
2. Six 2D observations resolve to six original IDs, three studies and three proposed identities; production remains 1,057.
3. Graphene is distinct from POCO graphite. Preparation lineage points to the actual study feedstock, never a chemically similar unrelated catalogue product.
4. A reviewed Cu-ETP alias resolves once within its supplier namespace; another supplier's grade is not automatically merged.
5. PBS/PBAT 70/30 is one blend with two component roles and unknown ratio basis. No invented wt% or inherited constituent properties.
6. Si X2 density does not prove semiconductor functionality. Candidate membership remains excluded; unknown doping is not undoped.
7. Layer count alone normally distinguishes states; different phase, doping or termination composition is not an alias. Unknown phase remains unknown.
8. MoS2 preparation anneal is not test temperature. Preserve the printed q/formula warning.
9. hBN model thickness and illustrated AFM apparent height remain distinct. N/m-to-GPa conversion adds a derived observation with explicit thickness.
10. Different MXene test methods retain methods and bounded states. Do not average incompatible values or create new chemical identities from test method alone.
11. Li/O-deficient LTO and nominal Li4Ti5O12 are not aliases; measured composition and nominal formula remain separate.
12. Alpha NiFe/CoFe precursors and operating gamma phases remain distinct and condition-linked. OER performance does not become a property of unactivated precursors or Nickel200. Reversible processing uses time-specific states, not graph cycles.
13. TEM dry-core, DLS hydrodynamic and BET measurements are distinct. Nanopores do not make micrometer grains nanoparticles. 2D and nano facets may overlap with evidence.
14. Conflicting same-scope claims stay visible but outside default confirmed counts; differences in temperature or method are not automatically conflicts.
15. Adding 100 temperatures/directions/predictions does not change the identity count. Reverse traversal returns the same assertion ID.
16. Reject component/lineage/taxonomy back edges, unsupported self-links and unknowns replaced with zero/defaults.
17. Missing provenance, withdrawn evidence or expired historical certificates cannot silently become current functional evidence; historical releases remain queryable.
18. Alias revision does not change identity. One-to-many split resolution returns ambiguous. The frozen 1,057 count and 3,953 deficit remain reproducible.
19. Domain coverage is nonadditive. Natural overage does not offset other categories. No eight-domain quota migration without a separate decision.
20. supported+proposed does not display as admitted, deployed or experimentally verified. Passing schema is not primary-source verification.
21. MP theoretical=false/ICSD links do not turn computed properties into experimental observations. Computed is_metal does not imply the legacy metal category.
22. MP/NOMAD formula-only matches remain candidate. Entry/task/provider IDs stay separate from canonical IDs; snapshot revisions and field-level provenance survive normalization.
23. Provider errors/missing data are not zero values. License unknown remains unknown. Canonical ID stays null and quota_credit=0 until admission.

Cases11–13 use research nominations from domain audits only; this package creates no production identities or new experimental values for them.
