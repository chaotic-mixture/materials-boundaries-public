# Two PBS/lignin simple-blend candidates

This packet proposes two composite identities, two injection-molded tensile
states and two selected Young's-modulus properties against experimental baseline
`7388976fa914399a210322d019e3081f201ff08f`. It adds no grade or source. Runtime catalogs,
formal-component pins and released main's 1081 admissions are unchanged.
Candidate preparation contributes zero formal admissions.

The source is Nnaemeka Ewurum and Armando G. McDonald (2025), *Lignin
Reinforcement in Polybutylene Succinate Copolymers*, Polymers 17, 194,
[DOI 10.3390/polym17020194](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym17020194).

Section 2.3 defines the Bxx simple-blend series separately from DCP-reactive
Rxx and records separately extruded 50 g formulations. Table 6 on PDF page 12
reports each selected physical blend's measured tensile result. The changed
lignin mass fractions distinguish these formulations from each other and from
existing B0 and B20; neither processing states nor repeated tests are additional
material identities. The lignin is Westvaco-supplied Indulin AT softwood kraft
lignin. The unresolved BioPBS FZ91PM/FZ91PB designation stays explicit, with no
qualified grade selected or invented.

Section 2.9 on page 5 gives ten tensile replicates, ASTM D1708, an Instron
5500R-1132 with Bluehill v3.3, an Epsilon model 3542 extensometer and 1 mm/min
crosshead speed. Table 6 footnote a defines mean +/- standard deviation and
its SD rounding convention. Superscript b is a Tukey grouping. No conversion
to SE, confidence intervals or bounds is made.

The selected specimens are injection-molded microtensile dog-bones. Section
2.3 describes 140 C strand extrusion and pelletization followed by Dynisco LMM
compounding at 100 rpm for 10 minutes at 140 C and injection molding. Do not
transfer the separate hot-pressed-disc route or DCP/acetone pretreatment to
these simple-blend tensile states. Numerical modulus fit window/estimator,
tensile-test temperature/RH and post-molding conditioning remain unknown.
The processing temperature is not a tensile-test temperature.
