# Three additional NBS inorganic crystal references

This packet contains three composition-level identities, three source-qualified
states and three crystallographic-density references under the existing material
contract. It reuses the registered NBS source unchanged and adds no source or
grade. It does not modify runtime catalogs or increase the released main count.

| Identity | Source-qualified sample | Density (g/cm^3) | Explicit density temperature | Density locator |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `mat_nbs1953_nh4cl` | J. T. Baker; ACS compliance; recrystallized by sublimation | 1.527 | 26 degrees C | Printed p. 61 / PDF p. 65 |
| `mat_nbs1953_licl` | Mallinckrodt sample labelled SDD; loosely packed for XRD | 2.074 | 25 degrees C | Printed p. 62 / PDF p. 66 |
| `mat_nbs1953_kf` | J. T. Baker; sodium impurity retained; dried then mounted with petrolatum | 2.524 | 26 degrees C | Printed p. 65 / PDF p. 69 |

The complete retained official PDF SHA-256 is
`003b872fbe7d403d38bbd1d752568ee92a96bdb010b6e381bdc82566929e2a6c`.
Actual images of one-based PDF pages 6, 63, 64, 65, 66, 68 and 69 were viewed
locally on 2026-10-16. These correspond to printed pages 2, 59, 60, 61, 62, 64
and 65. The source is Swanson and Tatge, *Standard X-ray Diffraction Powder
Patterns, Volume I*, NBS Circular 539 (1953),
[official PDF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/circ/nbscircular539v1.pdf).

All values are the source's crystallographic calculations from NBS experimental
lattice constants. They are not direct bulk-density measurements, powder-packing
densities, DFT predictions or newly recomputed results. NH4Cl has one formula
unit per simple cubic cell; LiCl and KF have four per NaCl-type cell. These cell
contents are not physical-specimen counts. Density-specific uncertainty,
pressure, total purity and independent repeat counts remain unknown.

KF retains the NBS approximately 0.1 percent sodium finding and the source's
separate discussion of NaF solid solution. No exact solid-solution composition,
percentage basis or pure/undoped claim is inferred. Its 220 degrees C drying is
preparation, distinct from the 26 degrees C density temperature. Petrolatum is
an XRD mounting aid and creates no additional material or composite-density
observation. LiCl's SDD label creates no grade, and loose packing does not change
the crystallographic-density interpretation. Neighboring material values and
other authors' comparison values are excluded.

The existing source-specific NIST reuse rationale and attribution are retained:
**Republished courtesy of the National Institute of Standards and Technology.**
Only selected NBS-authored factual values, bibliographic metadata and original
curation are included. PDFs, page images, raw structures and complete tables are
not bundled. All name locales deliberately use English fallback labels.
`independent_scientific_review` and `raw_data_reanalysis` remain false; every
property remains `catalog_only` with universal-bound and engineering-allowable
flags false. Publication has not been performed.
