# SPO-2 rigid polyurethane foam: one candidate

This packet proposes one polymer identity, one state and one apparent-density
reference against experimental base
`7388976fa914399a210322d019e3081f201ff08f`. It adds no grade or source. Candidate
preparation contributes **zero formal admissions** and does not modify runtime

The source is Ivdre et al. (2024), *Rigid Polyurethane Foams' Development and
Optimization from Polyols Based on Depolymerized Suberin and Tall Oil Fatty
Acids*, Polymers 16(7), 942,
[DOI 10.3390/polym16070942](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym16070942).

was reread on 2026-10-17; its SHA-256
`4e0c1a91a9ae26d814576961a3805f9371b46498d4861fb578e7a413bb4c726d`

rewriting or extending the historical LPO-1 review by implication.

SPO-2 uses synthesized suberinic-acid/tall-oil-derived polyols, unlike the existing
Lupranol-based LPO-1. Table 9 specifies 67 g SA-C_TOFA(50:50)/TMP and
33 g SA-E_ETOFA(50:50)/TMP; total water 0.5 g; Opteon 1100 24.3 g;
Polycat NP10 1.55 g; PC CAT TKA 30 0.5 g; L-6915 2.5 g; TCPP 20 g;
and pMDI 102 g. The table's footnotes specify TCPP at 8 wt.% of total foam mass
and NCO/OH 1.2. These are formulation inputs, not a final-composition assay.
SPO-2 is a study label, not a certified finished-material grade.

Table 10 reports **40.7 kg/m3 apparent density** for experimentally characterized
SPO-2. Section 2.8 cites ISO 845:2006. Section 2.7 describes a 30 by 30 by 10 cm
large mold and 24 h room-temperature cure. Density aggregation, uncertainty,
sample count, actual test temperature, humidity, pressure and specimen dimensions
remain unreported. Cup-test counts of three per formulation or 81 per series,
six compression cylinders, 10 degrees C thermal-conductivity testing and
25 degrees C polyol rheometry do not supply those missing density conditions.

The 45 kg/m3 optimization target and 40 kg/m3 compression-normalization density
are not substitutes for the selected measurement. Closed-cell content of
95 vol.% is a state fact, not total porosity. No compression modulus or other
property is appended. One selected SP-series representative is proposed;
SPO-1/SPO-3 and LPO-2/LPO-3 are not additional identities in this packet.

A separate source discrepancy is retained: Table 10 gives SPO-1 density
43.9 kg/m3, whereas Section 3.4 states an overall range of 40.7-43.5 kg/m3.
SPO-1 is excluded. The selected SPO-2 value agrees with the stated lower limit;
neither the conflicting value nor the prose is silently corrected.
