## Source qualifications retained in product records

Członka, Fischer Kerche, Motta Neves, Strąkowska and Strzelec (2021),
[DOI 10.3390/ijms222011203](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms222011203), report
three nominal bleached Curauá fiber loadings relative to total RPUF mass:
0.1, 0.5 and 1 wt%. Their Table 2 apparent-density means are respectively
38.4, 37.9 and 37.6 kg/m3. Section 3.2 specifies mass divided by volume,
ISO 845 and ten density samples per group.

The original superscripts 0.4/0.6/0.4 are called coefficient of variance, but
their unit is not explicit. The separate statistical groups are C/B/A. Literal
observations remain in the records; typed uncertainty remains null without
inferring percent, SD, confidence interval or zero uncertainty.

The source reports 24 h curing at room conditions and, in the thermal discussion,
at least seven days post-curing. The exact density measurement age is unresolved.
Density temperature, pressure, humidity and detailed conditioning remain unknown.
The mean density is not a mechanical or thermal measurement, and those tests'
conditions are not transferred. Nominal loading is not analyzed final
composition or validated cured-network stoichiometry. The title does not
establish a numerical bio-based fraction.

The article has an explicit CC BY 4.0 license, with author attribution and
transcription/curation notice preserved. The publisher PDF was downloaded,
hashed and visually checked by the producer team. Independent candidate review
reproduced the official XML bytes and factual joins; its publisher-PDF request
returned HTTP 403, so it did not independently confirm PDF bytes or page images.
The candidate's 22-input finite identity screen is reproducible but is not proof
of global chemical uniqueness or independent scientific replication.
