# Development history: v0.13.2

This was not a public release. The first public release is **0.16.0**; its
[scope](MIGRATION_v0.16.0.md) and [license notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
are authoritative for the public package.

This development milestone separated source provenance from permission to
redistribute source material. The NIST cryogenics homepage records a past SRD
origin; general website guidance was not sufficient evidence of product-specific
redistribution or relicensing terms.

The current public release records the more complete status: NIST lists the
collection as a curated data collection, formerly SRD 152. Reclassification
alone does not establish an express reuse grant. See [official sources and the
release decision](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md#nist-cryogenic-material-properties).

The five cryogenic coefficient datasets and every derived sample output are
omitted. Bibliographic references remain, with no public-domain/CC0 assertion.
The omission is a conservative choice pending clarification, not a legal
conclusion that redistribution is prohibited. Original code, documentation,
curation and synthetic demonstrations use MIT; third-party works retain their
rights. Historical coefficients and results are deliberately not reproduced in
this migration guide.
