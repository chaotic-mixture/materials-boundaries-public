# Initial v0.29.0 material coverage

The released registry has 21 distinct identities, 11 qualified grades, 21
source-scoped states and 21 selected properties. Category counts are inorganic: 8, metal: 8, polymer: 5.
There are 16 primary/evidence source records in this lane, with 16 newly
appended source records. Each listed identity has one selected property; this
is not a census of chemical families or an independent-replication count.

The eight inorganic identities comprise five study-specific ceramic formulations, two named glass materials and one bulk graphite. Together with eight metals and five printed-polymer formulations, these make the 21 admitted identities. This descriptive grouping does not equate source-scoped states with generic chemical-family constants.

The displayed numbers retain source units and precision. Always inspect the
linked record’s conditions, evidence class, statistical definition and limits.
No conversion, inference of ambient conditions, bound or engineering allowable
is implied.
