Two studies: reported tensile strength and temperature

Ten condition summaries from two different experiments. Read the protocol and statistic differences before the values.

Interpretation limits

Descriptive juxtaposition only. Matched conditions and statistical independence are not established. Common units, PA12/15 wt.% declarations and ±45° labels do not make the formulations, protocols or central statistics equivalent.

These are different commercial formulations. Declared PA12 and 15 wt.% carbon fiber are not an independent composition assay. A 100% infill setting does not establish measured zero porosity. Drying does not establish measured specimen moisture.

Actual moisture/RH, stress convention, stress-area basis and local strain rate remain unestablished. Source-cited standards are not verified compliance; nominal dimensions do not establish actual stress area. Unknown conditions are not treated as equal.

Temperature is a reported chamber condition, not a directly verified specimen reading; its uncertainty is unknown. 23 °C is not 25 °C. Even the shared 100 °C label does not establish matched conditioning. No rate ratio or merged condition row is inferred.

SD is reported dispersion, not SEM, CI, uncertainty of a median, an observed endpoint range, coverage, a hard bound or a complete uncertainty budget. Raw replicates, their independence and the SD formula are unverified; counts do not justify calculating a CI.

No interpolation, extrapolation, fit, pooling, normalization, deltas, ranking, significance, material prediction, design allowable or geometry-derived conversion. Agreement between transcriptions is not experimental replication or scientific validation.

Scientific wording, the new presentation and translations are machine-assisted and not independently scientifically or native-language reviewed. Transcription agreement verifies copying only.
