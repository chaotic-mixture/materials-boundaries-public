Preparation, geometry and protocol

Source-reported nominal dog-bone geometry: length 150 mm; gauge length 80 mm; thickness 3 mm; narrow/wide widths 10/20 mm; grip separation 110 mm. Crosshead-derived engineering strain uses the full 110 mm, including transition regions, not only the 80 mm gauge. Local strain rate, dimensional tolerances and measured stress cross-section are unknown. No stress is reconstructed from geometry.

Creality K1 MAX FFF; horizontal build; alternating +45°/−45° raster; 100% linear infill; five contours; 0.2 mm layers; 0.6 mm nozzle; nozzle/bed 265/100 °C; inner/outer wall speeds 60/60 mm/s; reported cooling 100%; reported specimen mass 6.42 g. Step-Lab UD08 tension with integrated thermal chamber. Filament diameter 1.75 mm; supplied spool mass 0.5 kg.

Atmosphere and pressure are unknown. Numerical relative humidity, specimen moisture, temperature sensor location, stability tolerance, and tensile heating rate are unknown.
